### "Latest" check

Copies of the same skills exist in `~/dev/claude-code-mods/.claude`, `~/dev/evolv-dq-ai-accelerator/.claude`,
and `~/dev/kkr_loan_dashboard/.claude`. A recursive diff of every skill, agent, command, rule, and context
against `~/.claude` found one difference: `kkr_loan_dashboard` holds an older `destructive-ops-guard`
(2026-09-20) than `~/.claude` (2026-10-04). `~/.claude` is therefore the newest copy of everything and is
what this repo contains. The `evolv-dq-ai-accelerator` hook copy of the destructive guard also differs; the
`~/.claude` version is the one with the test suite and is the one shipped.
