## CCAR-F (Architect, Foundations; also CCA-F)

Facts most sources agree on: scenario-based; 4 of 6 scenarios drawn at random; 60 questions; 120 minutes; scaled 720 of 1000; five domains weighted 27 / 20 / 20 / 18 / 15 (agentic orchestration, Claude Code config, prompting and structured output, tool design and MCP, context management and reliability). $125 since 2026-06-30.

### What candidates say is tested and hard
- Judgement calls over recall: when to use a subagent, retry versus fail, when to escalate to a human. Wrong options are realistic anti-patterns. [dev.to](https://dev.to/aws-builders/the-claude-certified-architect-exam-5-domains-6-scenarios-and-everything-you-need-to-know-4le3), [LinkedIn snippet](https://sg.linkedin.com/in/jazz)
- Claude Code configuration was the weakest area for one passer (69%), ahead of tool/MCP (70%) and context (73%). [verygood.ventures](https://verygood.ventures/blog/passing-the-claude-certified-architect-exam/)
- CLI specifics: flags, which hook to use, where user-level and project-level instruction files live. One passer called flags the hardest part because they had skipped them. [rogs.me](https://rogs.me/2026/07/how-i-got-claude-certified-and-how-you-can-too/)
- MCP config placement mistakes, such as putting team-wide servers in the wrong config file (a second-hand report; its page is excluded below, so treat as unconfirmed).
- Pacing: under two minutes per question, long dense scenarios, two plausible answers per item. One word such as "guaranteed" or "fastest" can flip the answer. [verygood.ventures](https://verygood.ventures/blog/passing-the-claude-certified-architect-exam/), [rogs.me](https://rogs.me/2026/07/how-i-got-claude-certified-and-how-you-can-too/)
- A scoring-configuration error that initially showed 700 instead of 755 was reported and corrected by Anthropic. [LinkedIn snippet](https://hk.linkedin.com/in/antonchenyitong) (single anecdote)

### Recommended study path
- Free Academy courses: Claude 101, Building with the Claude API, MCP intro and advanced, subagents, agent skills, Claude Code 101 and in Action, Cowork. [dev.to](https://dev.to/aws-builders/the-claude-certified-architect-exam-5-domains-6-scenarios-and-everything-you-need-to-know-4le3)
- Read Anthropic's exam guide several times until the domains and scenarios feel familiar. [LevelUp snippet](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/how-i-passed-the-claude-certified-architect-foundation-cca-f-exam-in-my-first-real-attempt-b6d24c60a058)
- Hands-on builds from the guide (see best practices). [vanja.io](https://vanja.io/claude-certification/)
- Study time claims range from 15-20 h (experienced) to 30-40 h (newer) to "40+ h". [cloudthat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/which-claude-certification-should-you-choose-associate-vs-developer-vs-architect), [search snippet](https://certdemand.com/guides/claude-certified-architect-guide)
- Timed practice runs, then careful review of misses; but practice items did not overlap the real exam, so rising practice scores can be memorisation. [verygood.ventures](https://verygood.ventures/blog/passing-the-claude-certified-architect-exam/)

### Best practices repeated across sources
- Build an end-to-end Agent SDK agent with error handling, sessions and subagents. [dev.to](https://dev.to/aws-builders/the-claude-certified-architect-exam-5-domains-6-scenarios-and-everything-you-need-to-know-4le3), [vanja.io](https://vanja.io/claude-certification/)
- Configure Claude Code on a real project: instruction-file hierarchy, path-scoped rules, a custom skill, an MCP server. [same two sources]
- Write MCP tool descriptions that keep similar tools distinct; return structured errors. [same]
- Build a schema-constrained extraction pipeline with validation-retry and the batch API. [same]
- Practice context management and explicit escalation rules. [vanja.io](https://vanja.io/claude-certification/)
- Read the full question first, flag and move on, prefer the option where Claude does more of the work unless the scenario says otherwise. [rogs.me](https://rogs.me/2026/07/how-i-got-claude-certified-and-how-you-can-too/)
- Use an AI tutor as a sparring partner, not as a readiness measure. [verygood.ventures](https://verygood.ventures/blog/passing-the-claude-certified-architect-exam/)
- Repo advice: find the deciding phrase in each question and choose the least-machinery fix that addresses the root cause. [paullarionov repo](https://github.com/paullarionov/claude-certified-architect)

## CCAR-P (Architect, Professional; also CCA-P)

Facts (prep-site, citing the July 2026 guide): 63 items, multiple-choice and multiple-response, 120 minutes, 720 of 1000, $175, seven domains: Integration 19, Solution Design and Architecture 17, Evaluation/Testing/Optimization 16, Governance/Safety/Risk 14, Stakeholder Communication and Lifecycle 14, Models/Prompting/Context 13, Developer Productivity and Operational Enablement 7. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccar-p/)

### What candidates say is tested and hard
- The three domains beyond Foundations (governance, stakeholder/lifecycle, developer enablement) are the unfamiliar part for pure builders; one vendor puts them at 35% together (14+14+7 = 35, consistent). [cloudthat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/which-claude-certification-should-you-choose-associate-vs-developer-vs-architect), [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccar-p/)
- Reported question themes (single anecdote, via search summary): least-privilege tool design, diagnosing a retrieval pipeline that returns confident wrong answers after a document refresh, explaining architecture to a CFO, a security reviewer and an engineer. [LevelUp CCAR-P snippet](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/i-passed-the-claude-certified-architect-professional-ccar-p-exam-in-two-days-of-prep-31e0bcbe04de)
- No first-hand detail beyond that was retrievable; the exam is under NDA.

### Recommended study path
- No prerequisite; Foundations is a suggested on-ramp, not required. Recommended background: 3+ years in architecture and 6+ months with Claude in production. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccar-p/), [official FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications)
- Official guide has only three sample questions and there is no official practice exam. [search summary](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/guided/anthropic-ccar-p/)
- Prep-site banks (Tutorials Dojo, Udemy, aguidetocloud) exist; quality unverified.

### Best practices repeated across sources
- Cover trade-off reasoning across accuracy, latency, cost, safety and maintainability. [search summary of Tutorials Dojo listing](https://tutorialsdojo.com/?p=40095)
- Prepare governance, risk and lifecycle material explicitly; builders tend to skip it. [cloudthat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/which-claude-certification-should-you-choose-associate-vs-developer-vs-architect)

## CCAO-F (Associate, Foundations)

Facts: audience is non-developers (ops, marketing, HR, PM, consultants); 60 items, 120 min, 720/1000, $99; no coding. Seven domains: Output Evaluation and Validation 21, Workflow Integration and Solution Design 16, Governance/Risk/Responsible Use 15, Prompting and Task Execution 14, Product and Model Selection 12, Configuration and Knowledge Management 12, Troubleshooting and Optimization 10. It is the only exam that does not count toward partner eligibility. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccao-f/), [official FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications)

### What candidates say is tested and hard
- Output evaluation (accuracy, hallucination, bias, when a human must review) is the heaviest and most-cited skill. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccao-f/)
- Responsible use content is where developers reportedly fail because they skip it. [cloudthat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/which-claude-certification-should-you-choose-associate-vs-developer-vs-architect)
- Product/model selection, Projects and connector configuration, and context limits are in scope (non-API). [search summary](https://dev.to/victoria_meisel_d65e0bad3/claude-certified-associate-foundations-ccao-f-preparation-material-for-certification-success-16p9)
- No first-hand pass report found.

### Recommended study path
- Partner Academy prep courses plus public Academy: Claude 101, Cowork, AI Fluency, AI Capabilities and Limitations. [vanja.io](https://vanja.io/claude-certification/), [academy.claude.com](https://academy.claude.com/courses)
- Start with the weakest-domain diagnostic from any bank, then weight time to output evaluation over prompt tricks. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccao-f/)

### Best practices repeated across sources
- Fix workflows, not single outputs; document configuration and knowledge sources. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccao-f/)
- Treat privacy and data sensitivity (regulated data) as first-class. [search summary](https://sandlabs.com.au/blog/claude-certification-guide)

## CCDV-F (Developer, Foundations)

Facts (prep-site citing July 2026 guide): 53 items, 120 min, 720/1000, $125; eight domains: Applications and Integration 33.1, Model Selection and Optimization 16.8, Agents and Workflows 14.7, Prompt and Context Engineering 11.0, Tools and MCPs 10.6, Security and Safety 8.1, Claude Code 3.1, Evaluation/Testing/Debugging 2.6. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)

### What candidates say is tested and hard
- Code-first questions: trace request and response flows, streaming events, error categories, state and permissions. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)
- Multi-response items: some say how many to pick; read every option and pick the best. [search summary of LinkedIn posts](https://br.linkedin.com/in/rafaellaeber)
- Weight is on API mechanics and integration, not on Claude Code (3.1%), which differs sharply from CCAR-F.
- Security: prompt injection, untrusted input, least privilege, secrets handling. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)

### Recommended study path
- Developer Foundations course (code-first, API and Agent SDK) then Architect Foundations as the next step. [cloudthat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/which-claude-certification-should-you-choose-associate-vs-developer-vs-architect)
- Academy: Claude Platform 101, Building with the Claude API, MCP intro/advanced, subagents, agent skills. [vanja.io](https://vanja.io/claude-certification/), [academy.claude.com](https://academy.claude.com/courses)
- Study in blueprint-weight order starting with Applications and Integration. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)

### Best practices repeated across sources
- Separate fixed workflows from model-driven agents; compare models on measured output and token use; keep permissions separate from untrusted text. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)
- Debug with logs and error categories before blaming the model. [aguidetocloud](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)
- Read proctoring rules ahead; second monitors and visible phones cost people seats (one prep-site claim, low confidence).

## Skill ideas

Claude-platform competencies the sources say practitioners need. For a skill library, each is a candidate skill or checklist.

1. **Agent orchestration decisions** (workflow vs agent, coordinator/subagent split, retry vs fail vs escalate). Heaviest CCAR-F domain (27%) and 14.7% of CCDV-F. [dev.to](https://dev.to/aws-builders/the-claude-certified-architect-exam-5-domains-6-scenarios-and-everything-you-need-to-know-4le3), [aguidetocloud CCDV-F](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)
2. **MCP tool design**: distinct descriptions, structured errors, config scope (project vs user). Hard for candidates; also 10.6% of CCDV-F. [vanja.io](https://vanja.io/claude-certification/), [rogs.me](https://rogs.me/2026/07/how-i-got-claude-certified-and-how-you-can-too/)
3. **Claude Code configuration**: instruction-file hierarchy, path rules, hooks, skills, CLI flags, CI use. Weakest area for a passer; flags cited as hardest. [verygood.ventures](https://verygood.ventures/blog/passing-the-claude-certified-architect-exam/), [rogs.me](https://rogs.me/2026/07/how-i-got-claude-certified-and-how-you-can-too/)
4. **Structured output pipelines**: JSON schema, nullable fields, validate-retry loops, batch API. 20% of CCAR-F. [dev.to](https://dev.to/aws-builders/the-claude-certified-architect-exam-5-domains-6-scenarios-and-everything-you-need-to-know-4le3)
5. **Context and reliability management**: extracted facts, scratchpad files, subagent delegation, human escalation rules. [vanja.io](https://vanja.io/claude-certification/)
6. **Output evaluation and validation**: spotting hallucination, bias, inconsistency; deciding when human review is required. Biggest CCAO-F domain (21%) and an Evaluation domain in CCAR-P (16%). [aguidetocloud CCAO-F](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccao-f/), [aguidetocloud CCAR-P](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccar-p/)
7. **Governance, safety and responsible use**: data sensitivity, least privilege, policy. Cited as where non-architects fail; 14% of CCAR-P and 15% of CCAO-F. [cloudthat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/which-claude-certification-should-you-choose-associate-vs-developer-vs-architect), [aguidetocloud CCAR-P](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccar-p/)
8. **Security against prompt injection and untrusted input**: separate permissions from text, secrets handling. 8.1% of CCDV-F. [aguidetocloud CCDV-F](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)
9. **Model selection and cost/token optimisation** (Opus/Sonnet/Haiku, measured trade-offs). 16.8% of CCDV-F; 12% of CCAO-F. [aguidetocloud CCDV-F](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/), [aguidetocloud CCAO-F](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccao-f/)
10. **Claude API integration mechanics**: streaming events, error handling, batch, request tracing. 33.1% of CCDV-F. [aguidetocloud CCDV-F](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccdv-f/)
11. **Architecture communication and lifecycle** (explain trade-offs to CFO/security/engineers; rollout and enablement). CCAR-P-only domains. [LevelUp CCAR-P snippet](https://levelup.gitconnected.com/i-passed-the-claude-certified-architect-professional-ccar-p-exam-in-two-days-of-prep-31e0bcbe04de), [aguidetocloud CCAR-P](https://www.aguidetocloud.com/cert-tracker/anthropic-ccar-p/)
12. **RAG and retrieval diagnosis** (stale index after refresh, confident wrong answers). CCAR-P scope. [search summary](https://tutorialsdojo.com/?p=40095)
13. **Exam technique** (read the full stem first, constraint words, flag-and-move, "when not to use Claude"). Repeated by several passers. [rogs.me](https://rogs.me/2026/07/how-i-got-claude-certified-and-how-you-can-too/), [verygood.ventures](https://verygood.ventures/blog/passing-the-claude-certified-architect-exam/)

## Conflicts and staleness

- **Eligibility**: official pages say Partner Network employees only, partner email, personal emails rejected ([FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications), [launch post](https://claude.com/blog/four-role-based-claude-certifications)). Older or promotional pages (e.g. the dev.to article's tone and several prep sites) read as though anyone can sit the exam. Treat open-to-all claims as stale.
- **Practice exam**: the official FAQ says the old practice exam was retired in the move to Pearson ([FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications)); the dev.to article and June-July passers still describe taking it ([dev.to](https://dev.to/aws-builders/the-claude-certified-architect-exam-5-domains-6-scenarios-and-everything-you-need-to-know-4le3), [verygood.ventures](https://verygood.ventures/blog/passing-the-claude-certified-architect-exam/)). The "score 900+ on the practice exam" advice is from the retired era. Passers also say practice and real items did not overlap.
- **Validity**: verygood.ventures (June) says the cert lasts six months; the FAQ says 12 months, with pre-2026-06-30 certs extended from 6 to 12 ([FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications)). The FAQ is newer; use it.
- **Price**: some sources quote CCAR-F at $99; FAQ says $125 for registrations on or after 2026-06-30. Partner tier discounts (50%; Global Premier 100% through 2026-12-31) are rarely mentioned by third parties.
- **CCAR-F weights and item count**: aguidetocloud's CCAR-F page says Anthropic does not publish weights or counts, while the same site's other pages cite weights from the July 2026 guide, and most others print 27/20/20/18/15 and 60 items. claudelog (updated 2026-10-24) also says weights, counts, and prices are not stated. Confirm against the official guide.
- **Developer prerequisites**: cloudthat lists 1-5 years engineering and 6 months LLM experience as a requirement; the aguidetocloud page calls them recommendations, and the official FAQ lists no formal prerequisite.
- **Exam codes**: CCAR-F/CCAR-P on the Pearson page versus CCA-F/CCA-P in community usage; some prep sites use CCA-A and CCD-F for Associate and Developer. [claudelog](https://claudelog.com/faqs/what-are-claude-certifications/)
- **Which is hardest**: one passer called CCAR-F the hardest of the four; cloudthat calls CCAR-P governance content the unfamiliar part. No data to settle it. [LinkedIn snippet](https://hk.linkedin.com/in/antonchenyitong), [cloudthat](https://www.cloudthat.com/resources/blog/which-claude-certification-should-you-choose-associate-vs-developer-vs-architect)
- **Course catalogue drift**: the Skilljar page lists 23 courses (with "Claude Platform 101" and an older "Building with the Claude API") while academy.claude.com lists 27 (adds Claude Tag, AI-native SDLC playbook, human-agent teams). Neither page maps courses to certs, and the official FAQ says it does not either; any "course X is required for cert Y" claim is the author's inference.
- **Likely-stale product details**: older posts reference the Bedrock/Vertex courses as optional prep; the exam blueprints as reported do not weight them.
